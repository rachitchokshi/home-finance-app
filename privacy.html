Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

home-finance is a private bookkeeping tool used by a single household. Only that household's own Google accounts sign in to it.

What it accesses

Read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly), used to find purchase receipts and order emails and match them to the household's card transactions. It cannot send, delete or change email.

Where data goes

  • Email content and the resulting ledger are stored only on the household's own computer.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Selected receipt text may be sent to an AI model provider solely to extract item names and prices; it is not used for any other purpose.

Google API Services User Data Policy

home-finance's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Any account can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored sign-in token is then deleted.
